Engineered, not styled

Four decisions you can hear

  1. 01

    One driver, milled from a single billet

    The motor housing is cut from one piece of aluminium rather than glued from three. Nothing rings, nothing loosens, and the midrange stays where we put it after a decade of daily use.

  2. 02

    Tuned by ear, then proved on the rig

    Every voicing starts with the same engineers and the same reference playlist they have argued over since the print works in 2016. Only once they agree does the measurement rig get to confirm it — never the other way around.

  3. 03

    A chassis that refuses to join in

    Damping is layered, not sprayed: a constrained viscoelastic core between two machined shells drops cabinet resonance below the noise floor of the room you are sitting in.

  4. 04

    Serviceable down to the last screw

    Pads, cables, drivers and boards are all replaceable with a single hex key. Parts stay stocked in Lisbon for ten years after a product leaves the catalogue, because the alternative is landfill with a nice finish.
